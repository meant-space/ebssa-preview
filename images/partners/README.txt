Partner logos go here.

The marquee under the hero currently shows partner NAMES because no logo
files exist in any material supplied so far. The component already supports
logos: drop a file in this folder and change the marquee item in both
index.html and ar/index.html from

    <b>UNICEF</b>
to
    <b><img src="images/partners/unicef.svg" alt="UNICEF"></b>

(use ../images/partners/... on the Arabic page).

Logos are shown at 34px tall, muted, and come to full colour on hover.
SVG is preferred; a transparent PNG at 3x the display height also works.

Before publishing a partner's logo, confirm EBSSA has permission. UNICEF,
WHO and most UN bodies require written authorisation for use of their marks,
and that authorisation is usually specified in the project agreement.
